### Risk 4. Tariffs: replacement tariffs and an unresolved refund

Replacement tariffs followed, and refunds of tariffs already paid were unresolved as of March 2026. Hershey's outlook excludes any refund, so a refund is upside; new tariffs are downside.

- **Supreme Court ruling, February 2026 (Learning Resources v. Trump):** ([[02 Atomic Notes/Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs in February 2026|note]]) "Last month, the Supreme Court ruled that the International Emergency Economic Powers Act" / "did not give President Donald Trump the authority to impose sweeping tariffs in a series of 2025 executive orders."
