# Milestone 2: Driver Justifications, Hershey (HSY)

Team: Chris Lester. Date: 2026-10-02. Forecast years: FY2026 to FY2028. Dollars in millions.

The forecast columns of the Q&D workbook (Detail Data tab) are FactSet consensus estimates (source note [[01 Sources/S8 FactSet consensus estimates for Hershey]], exported 2026-10-04). This memo states what each driver in that consensus implies and why we accept it, or where we would adjust it, naming the historical figure or benchmark behind our view. The export carries every row the sheet types, income statement through balance sheet and capex, so no forecast cell is ours; the checks we apply to it are stated at the end.

## 1. Revenue growth: consensus 5.2 percent in FY2026, 2.5 in FY2027, 2.6 in FY2028

FY2025 net sales were 11,692.6, up 4.4 percent on FY2024 ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 net sales growth 4.4 percent]], source [[01 Sources/S1 Hershey Form 10-K FY2025]]). Management guides FY2026 net sales growth of 4.5 to 5.0 percent ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2026 guidance net sales growth 4.5 to 5.0 percent]], source [[01 Sources/S2 Hershey Q2 2026 results press release]]). The US chocolate category grows about 2.5 percent a year ([[02 Atomic Notes/Chocolate industry revenue growth about 2.5 percent a year]]) and confectionery volume fell about 10 percent in Q2 2026 ([[02 Atomic Notes/Q2 2026 confectionery volume down about 10 percent]]), so growth is price-led. FY2026 consensus sits at the top of guidance and FY2027 and FY2028 fade to the category rate; we accept it. Any year above 5 percent is the one we would question, and FY2026 is just above it (5.2 percent), so we note that the year leans on price.

## 2. EBIT margin: consensus 20.5 percent in FY2026, 22.8 in FY2027, 23.4 in FY2028

FY2025 operating margin was 12.3 percent, down from 25.9 percent in FY2024 and 22.9 percent in FY2023, because cocoa costs peaked ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 operating margin 12.3 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2024 operating margin 25.9 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2023 operating margin 22.9 percent]]). Management guides about 400 basis points of improvement in FY2026 as lower cocoa flows through ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2026 margin improvement guided about 400 basis points]], source [[01 Sources/S3 Hershey Q2 2026 earnings call transcript]]), and cocoa is down more than 70 percent from its peak ([[02 Atomic Notes/Cocoa down more than 70 percent from late-2024 highs]]). Consensus recovers to 20.5 percent in FY2026, more than the guided 400 basis points, and to 23.4 percent by FY2028, still below the pre-2025 level; we accept it and would challenge any path back to 25 percent.

## 3. Reinvestment: consensus capex 3.6 to 4.0 percent of revenue; consensus working capital 7.0 to 10.4 percent of revenue

FY2025 capital expenditures were 454.6, or 3.9 percent of net sales ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 capex 3.9 percent of net sales]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 capital expenditures 455 million dollars]]), down from 605.9 in FY2024 as the ERP and capacity program wound down. We accept consensus capex near 4 percent of revenue (FY2026 guidance is 425 to 475). Consensus also carries current assets and liabilities, so the change in net working capital is consensus too; net working capital was 577.0 at FY2025, 4.9 percent of net sales, and consensus runs higher, 7.0 percent in FY2026 rising to 10.4 percent, because it builds cash inside current assets; we accept it and note that it makes the change in NWC a drag on FCFF. D&A follows the sheet's formula, the FY2025 ratio of D&A to capex applied to each year's capex ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 depreciation and amortization 504 million dollars]]).

## Checks we apply to the consensus

- Tax rate: consensus tax expense over consensus pre-tax income is 25.9 to 25.3 percent, near FY2025's effective rate of 27.3 percent (330.9 of taxes on 1,214.2 of pre-tax income; FY2024's 10.2 percent was depressed by a one-time item). Source [[01 Sources/S1 Hershey Form 10-K FY2025]].
- Gross margin: consensus is 41.1 to 44.2 percent, between FY2025's 33.5 percent and FY2024's 47.3 percent ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 gross margin 33.5 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2024 gross margin 47.3 percent]]).
- Net income: we type the GAAP row; FactSet's headline Net Income row is non-GAAP and runs higher.
- Interest expense and D&A: the sheet computes them (interest from long-term debt and the cost of debt on the WACC tab, Module 3).
