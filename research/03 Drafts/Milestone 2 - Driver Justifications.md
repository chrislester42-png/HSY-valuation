# Milestone 2: Driver Justifications, Hershey (HSY)

Team: Chris Lester. Date: 2026-09-30. Forecast years: FY2026 to FY2028. Dollars in millions.

These three drivers feed the forecast columns of the Q&D workbook (Detail Data tab). Each one names the historical figure or benchmark behind it and the source note it comes from.

## 1. Revenue growth: 4.5 percent a year

FY2025 net sales were 11,692.6, up 4.4 percent on FY2024 ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 net sales growth 4.4 percent]], source [[01 Sources/S1 Hershey Form 10-K FY2025]]). Management guides FY2026 net sales growth of 4.5 to 5.0 percent ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2026 guidance net sales growth 4.5 to 5.0 percent]], source [[01 Sources/S2 Hershey Q2 2026 results press release]]). We take the bottom of the range and hold it for three years, because the growth is price-led while confectionery volume fell about 10 percent in Q2 2026 ([[02 Atomic Notes/Q2 2026 confectionery volume down about 10 percent]]), and the US chocolate category grows about 2.5 percent a year ([[02 Atomic Notes/Chocolate industry revenue growth about 2.5 percent a year]]).

## 2. EBIT margin: 16.0 percent

FY2025 operating margin was 12.3 percent, down from 25.9 percent in FY2024 and 22.9 percent in FY2023, because cocoa costs peaked ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 operating margin 12.3 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2024 operating margin 25.9 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2023 operating margin 22.9 percent]]). Management guides about 400 basis points of margin improvement in FY2026 as lower cocoa flows through ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2026 margin improvement guided about 400 basis points]], source [[01 Sources/S3 Hershey Q2 2026 earnings call transcript]]). 12.3 plus 4.0 is 16.3; we round down to 16.0 and hold it, below the pre-2025 level, because pricing has not fully recovered the cocoa cost and volume is falling. Gross margin is held at 38.0 percent, between FY2025's 33.5 percent and FY2024's 47.3 percent ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 gross margin 33.5 percent]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2024 gross margin 47.3 percent]]).

## 3. Reinvestment: capex 4.0 percent of revenue, net working capital held at 4.9 percent of revenue

FY2025 capital expenditures were 454.6, or 3.9 percent of net sales ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 capex 3.9 percent of net sales]], [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 capital expenditures 455 million dollars]]), down from 605.9 in FY2024 as the ERP and capacity program wound down. We hold 4.0 percent. Net working capital (current assets less current liabilities) was 577.0 at FY2025, 4.9 percent of net sales; we hold that ratio, so the change in NWC grows with revenue. D&A follows the sheet's formula, the FY2025 ratio of D&A to capex applied to each year's capex ([[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 depreciation and amortization 504 million dollars]]).

## Other inputs the sheet needs

- Tax rate: 25 percent of pre-tax income (EBIT less interest). FY2025's effective rate was 27.3 percent (330.9 of taxes on 1,214.2 of pre-tax income); FY2024's 10.2 percent was depressed by a one-time item. Source [[01 Sources/S1 Hershey Form 10-K FY2025]].
- Interest expense: the sheet computes it from long-term debt and the cost of debt on the WACC tab (Module 3).
- Cash and long-term debt: held at FY2025 levels. No net borrowing assumed.
