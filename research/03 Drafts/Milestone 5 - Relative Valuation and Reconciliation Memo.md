Hershey trades at [[02 Atomic Notes/Hershey discount to peer average 26.9, 6.4, and 10.8 percent|a discount to the peer average on all three multiples]], deepest on EV/EBITDA and smallest on P/E. Two things drive it.

The average is pulled up by two premium names. [[02 Atomic Notes/Lindt multiples 19.08x EBITDA, 23.79x earnings, 4.16x sales|Lindt]] and [[02 Atomic Notes/Tootsie Roll multiples 25.31x EBITDA, 29.87x earnings, 3.74x sales|Tootsie Roll]] trade at 19.08x and 25.31x EBITDA. Against Mondelez, the one peer of Hershey's scale, Hershey trades [[02 Atomic Notes/Hershey premium to Mondelez 12.9, 9.7, and 29.2 percent|at a premium on every multiple]].

The market is pricing cocoa risk. Hershey's operating margin fell from [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2024 operating margin 25.9 percent|25.9 percent in FY2024]] to [[02 Atomic Notes/FY2025 operating margin 12.3 percent|12.3 percent in FY2025]] as cocoa costs rose ([[01 Sources/S1 Hershey Form 10-K FY2025|S1]]), and in Q2 2026 confectionery volume [[02 Atomic Notes/Q2 2026 confectionery volume down about 10 percent|fell about 10 percent]] while prices [[02 Atomic Notes/Q2 2026 price increase about 14 percent|rose about 14 percent]] ([[01 Sources/S3 Hershey Q2 2026 earnings call transcript|S3]]). A buyer paying today's multiple is not yet paying for the margin recovery our forecast assumes.

## 4. Reconciling the multiples with the DCF

| Method | Value per share | Against $160.19 |
|---|---|---|
| DCF, growth in perpetuity | $218.59 | 36.5% above |
| DCF, exit multiple | $193.02 | 20.5% above |
| EV/Sales, 3.42x FY2028 revenue | $213.50 | 33.3% above |
| P/E, 25.0x FY2028 NOPAT | $261.19 | 63.1% above |
| EV/EBITDA, 18.87x FY2028 EBITDA | $323.76 | 102.1% above |

Notes for this table: [[02 Atomic Notes/Equity value per share 218.59 dollars by growth in perpetuity|DCF by growth]], [[02 Atomic Notes/Equity value per share 193.02 dollars by exit multiple|DCF by multiple]], [[02 Atomic Notes/DCF values 36.5 and 20.5 percent above share price|DCF against the price]], [[02 Atomic Notes/Peer multiples imply 213.50 to 323.76 dollars a share|implied values per share]], [[02 Atomic Notes/Peer multiple values 33.3 to 102.1 percent above share price|multiples against the price]], [[02 Atomic Notes/HSY share price 160.19 dollars on 5 October 2026|share price]].

All five methods put Hershey above its price. They disagree on how far, and the gap has two sources.

**Which multiple.** Our DCF's exit multiple is [[02 Atomic Notes/Exit multiple 13.02x 2028 EBITDA|13.02x EBITDA]], close to Hershey's own [[02 Atomic Notes/Hershey multiples 13.8x EBITDA, 23.4x earnings, 3.05x sales|13.8x]] and Mondelez's [[02 Atomic Notes/Mondelez multiples 12.22x EBITDA, 21.34x earnings, 2.36x sales|12.22x]]. The peer average is [[02 Atomic Notes/Peer average multiples 18.87x EBITDA, 25.0x earnings, 3.42x sales|18.87x]]. Applying 18.87x instead of 13.02x to the same FY2028 EBITDA of 3,554.2 is most of the difference between [[02 Atomic Notes/Equity value per share 193.02 dollars by exit multiple|$193.02]] and [[02 Atomic Notes/Peer multiples imply 213.50 to 323.76 dollars a share|$323.76]]. The peer average prices the growth and quality of Lindt and Tootsie Roll; our [[02 Atomic Notes/Perpetual growth rate 3.0 percent|3.0 percent]] perpetual growth does not assume them.

**Which year.** The workbook applies each peer multiple to FY2028 forecast figures, after the margin recovery. The DCF discounts those same years back to [[02 Atomic Notes/Valuation date 5 October 2026 with 23.9 percent stub|October 5, 2026]].

**Which we trust.** The DCF. It states its growth and risk assumptions, and our Module 4 sensitivity tables show what each one is worth. The multiples are a cross-check, and the cross-check agrees on direction: Hershey is worth more than its price. EV/Sales, the multiple least affected by Hershey's cocoa-hit margins, gives [[02 Atomic Notes/Peer multiples imply 213.50 to 323.76 dollars a share|$213.50]], between our two DCF values.

## 6. The call

[[02 Atomic Notes/Call is Buy below 189 and avoid above 218 dollars|Buy below $189, avoid above $218.]] $189 is the [[02 Atomic Notes/Probability-weighted value 218.29 and 189.28 dollars a share|weighted value by exit multiple]], the lower of our two; at a price below it, the stock is cheap even on the more conservative terminal value. $218 is the [[02 Atomic Notes/Probability-weighted value 218.29 and 189.28 dollars a share|weighted value by growth in perpetuity]]; above it, there is no upside left on either method. At [[02 Atomic Notes/HSY share price 160.19 dollars on 5 October 2026|$160.19]], Hershey is below both: Buy.
