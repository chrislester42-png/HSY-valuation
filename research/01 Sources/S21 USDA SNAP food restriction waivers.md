# SNAP Food Restriction Waivers

## What it is
USDA's table of states with an approved SNAP food restriction waiver, with each state's restricted items and implementation date. Publication date is the page's last update.

## Key takeaways
- 23 states listed; four approvals vacated by a court.
- Candy limits in effect in seven states during 2026; three more start November 1, 2026; Kansas and Missouri February 15, 2027; Nebraska adds candy March 1, 2027.
