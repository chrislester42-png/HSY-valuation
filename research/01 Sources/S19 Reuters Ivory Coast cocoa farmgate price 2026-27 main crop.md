# Ivory Coast sets cocoa farmgate price at 1,200 CFA francs per kg for 2026/27 main crop, official says

## What it is
Reuters news report, republished by CNBC Africa, on Ivory Coast's cocoa farmgate price for the 2026/27 main crop, with London cocoa prices through 2026.

## Key takeaways
- Ivory Coast kept the farmgate price at 1,200 CFA francs per kg for the 2026/27 main crop; harvests run to February 28, 2027.
- The price was cut to 1,200 CFA francs in March 2026 for the mid-crop, from a record 2,800.
- London cocoa rose from about £2,100 in early March 2026 to about £3,800 by late June and above £4,800 by September 1.

## Direct quotes
> Ivory Coast has maintained the fixed farmgate price paid to cocoa farmers at 1,200 CFA francs (around $2.12) per kg
>
> Cocoa traded on global markets LCCc2 at about £2,100 ($2,840) per metric ton in early March,
>
> Prices have since climbed above £4,800 ($6,500) per ton.
