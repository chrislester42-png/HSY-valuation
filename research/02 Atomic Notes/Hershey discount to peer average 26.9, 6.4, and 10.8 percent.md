# Hershey discount to peer average 26.9, 6.4, and 10.8 percent

Hershey trades at a 26.9 percent discount to the peer average on EV to EBITDA, 6.4 percent on P/E, and 10.8 percent on EV to sales.

It is The Call's largest premium or discount fact (26.9 percent on EV/EBITDA). The memo gives two drivers: Lindt and Tootsie Roll pull the average up, and the market is pricing cocoa risk, with operating margin down from 25.9 to 12.3 percent and Q2 2026 volume down about 10 percent as prices rose about 14 percent.
