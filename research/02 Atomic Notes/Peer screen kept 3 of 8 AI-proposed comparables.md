# Peer screen kept 3 of 8 AI-proposed comparables

Of eight comparable companies Claude (Claude Code) proposed on 2026-10-07, the team kept three, Mondelez, Lindt and Sprüngli, and Tootsie Roll, and dropped five: Nestlé, Mars, Ferrero, General Mills, and J.M. Smucker.

The kept three are the peer set behind every multiple on The Call. The test was the module's: similar risk, growth, and cash flow, not a shared industry label. Reasons: Mondelez, chocolate and biscuits under global brands, US-listed; Lindt, chocolate is its whole business, branded and premium-priced; Tootsie Roll, US confectionery through the same retail channels. Nestlé, confectionery is a small part of a far larger food and beverage business; Mars and Ferrero, private, so no market multiples; General Mills, cereal, meals, and pet food with little confectionery; J.M. Smucker, coffee, pet food, and spreads lead its sales.
