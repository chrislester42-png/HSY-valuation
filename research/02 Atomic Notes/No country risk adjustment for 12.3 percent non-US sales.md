# No country risk adjustment for 12.3 percent non-US sales

The WACC carries no country risk adjustment for the 12.3 percent of Hershey's fiscal 2025 net sales made outside the United States.

If that share grew, or were concentrated in high-risk countries, a blended country premium would raise the cost of equity.
