# Perpetual growth rate 3.0 percent

The DCF assumes Hershey's free cash flow grows 3.0 percent a year forever after 2028.

It sets the growth-in-perpetuity terminal value, 92.3 percent of that method's enterprise value, so it is one of the two inputs that decide the answer. It stays below the 5.28 percent risk-free rate, as a stable growth rate must. Half a point lower, 2.5 percent, is the whole 25.57 dollar gap between our two methods.
