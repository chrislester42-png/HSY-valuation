# FY2024 effective tax rate 10.2 percent

Hershey's effective tax rate in fiscal 2024 was 10.2 percent: 252.7 million dollars of income taxes on 2,473.9 million dollars of pre-tax income.

The Milestone 2 memo sets this year aside as depressed by a one-time item, so it is not used as a benchmark for the forecast tax rate. If the item recurs, the forecast tax rate would be too high.
