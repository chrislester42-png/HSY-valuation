# Hershey debt to equity 14.37 percent

Hershey's debt-to-equity ratio at market value of equity is 14.37 percent: 4,681 million dollars of long-term debt on 32,582.6 million dollars of equity.

Used to relever the industry beta in the Hamada cross-check. It is far below the Food Processing average of 43.73 percent, which is one reason Hershey's beta sits low.
