# WACC tax rate 22.96 percent

The tax rate on Hershey's WACC tab is 22.96 percent: fiscal 2025 income taxes of 330.9 million dollars over operating income of 1,441.5 million dollars.

It sets the tax shield on debt (the after-tax cost of debt) and relevers the Hamada beta. It is lower than the 27.3 percent effective rate on pre-tax income because it divides by operating income, as the sheet's NOPAT row does.
