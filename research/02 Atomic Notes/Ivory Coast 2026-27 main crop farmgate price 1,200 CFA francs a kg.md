# Ivory Coast 2026-27 main crop farmgate price 1,200 CFA francs a kg

Ivory Coast kept the fixed farmgate price paid to cocoa farmers at 1,200 CFA francs (around $2.12) per kg for the 2026/27 main crop season, announced September 1, 2026.

A low farmgate price can cut farmers' investment and future supply, which would push cocoa up later (Risk 1).
