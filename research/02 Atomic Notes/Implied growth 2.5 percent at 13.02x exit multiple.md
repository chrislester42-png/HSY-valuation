# Implied growth 2.5 percent at 13.02x exit multiple

An exit multiple of 13.02x is the same as growing Hershey's free cash flow at 2.5 percent a year forever after 2028.

It is half a point below the 3.0 percent we assume, and that half point is the whole 25.57 dollar gap between our two methods.
