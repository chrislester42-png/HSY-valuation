# Scenario weights 25, 50, and 25 percent

The Call weights the Module 4 cases 25 percent bull, 50 percent base, and 25 percent bear.

The weights turn the bull, base, and bear table into the probability-weighted value per share, and they are where The Call's sliders start. The base case is the most likely, so it carries half; the upside and downside are seen as equally likely, so each carries a quarter.
