# Regression beta standard error 0.195 and R-squared 0.006

The 60-month regression that gives Hershey's raw beta of 0.1153 has a standard error of 0.195 and an R-squared of 0.006.

With a standard error larger than the beta itself, the raw figure cannot be told apart from zero. This is the memo's reason for using the adjusted beta instead.
